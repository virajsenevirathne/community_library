# Code of conduct

Reading Joey is made for daycares, schools and community shelves, by people
who care about children having books. We want taking part to be welcoming for
everyone, whatever their background, identity or experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and non-developers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give and take feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Keep other people's personal details, and any library's data, private.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any behaviour that puts children or families at risk.

## What happens

Report a problem privately to the maintainer (the address on the GitHub
profile). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments,
close issues, or block anyone who doesn't follow this code, in this project's
spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and chats).
